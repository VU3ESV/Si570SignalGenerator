Si570 hardware

These must match your Si570 part number. Changing the I²C address, start-up frequency or register bank re-initialises the chip and drops the external-reference calibration.

Si5351 hardware

CLK0–CLK2 of a Si5351 on the same I²C bus become outputs next to the Si570. CLK0 and CLK1 share a PLL, so only one of them can be above 100 MHz at a time; CLK2 has its own.

Network

Admin password

Protects this page, every change and firmware updates. User name admin. Lost it? Type auth clear on the USB serial console.

Firmware update

Choose .pio/build/esp32/firmware.bin from a build. The generator reboots once the update is written. PlatformIO can also update over the network: pio run -e esp32-ota -t upload.
